I’m a senior software engineer focused on authentication systems, API design, and building secure, scalable products. I currently work at Chime, and previously built systems at Panopta, Bank of America Merrill Lynch, AT\&T, the Chicago Cubs, and Medtelligent. I started my career as a graphic designer, which still shapes how I approach engineering with clean code, clear intent, and experiences that actually feel good to use.

Outside of work, I’m into gastronomy, photography, gardening, and snowboarding.

My professional path has ranged from financial market data and network monitoring to ticketing platforms, real time dashboards, and secure risk systems. On the design side, I’ve worked on branding, web design/development, and visual identity for Elektro, MMM’s Gourmet Ice Cream, and Koita Foods.

I’ve also built and contributed to projects like Sunscape, 180 Designs, DelusionFX, Northtrack: NAXP, Nano Labs, Timedish, and Webseam. Each one has been a different experiment in community, product, or platform.
